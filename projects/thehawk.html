THE HAWK SEASON ONE – PREMIERE & PRE-RECEPTION (NETFLIX)

On the back nine of his career, golf legend Lonnie “The Hawk” Hawkins chases one final major... and drags everyone he loves into the chaos.

As Lead Experiential Designer, I owned the 3D design and execution of the premiere and pre-reception, collaborating closely with Lead Graphic Designer Lina Cheng and Creative Director Alex Mutch. I designed the scenic assets and environmental touchpoints for the carpet, curated furniture, décor, and greens, and developed multiple site and permitting plans to bring the experience to life.

I also mapped guest flows across a range of audiences – from invited guests and influencers to key talent, cast, and BOH staff – ensuring the space was both visually immersive and operationally functional. From developing fabrication-ready designs and working closely with our fabricators to overseeing the details through install and on-site execution, I helped carry the experience from concept through build and premiere night.
